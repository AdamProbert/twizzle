# Parts list

Everything that goes into one Twizzle.

## Electronics

| Part | Qty | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ESP32-C3 SuperMini | 1 | 18 x 22.5 mm. Sits in a frame on the tray floor, under the wand. A dab of hot glue holds it. |
| 28BYJ-48 5 V stepper | 1 | From a 5-pack with ULN2003 boards. Snip the two mounting ears off. The tray and dome clamp it, and its blue wire cover keys it so it can't turn. |
| ULN2003 driver board (green) | 1 | About 35 x 32 mm, comes with the motor. Two M3 screws into inserts, the other two corners sit on pins. |
| 12 mm tactile switch with round cap | 1 | Front of the dome. |
| USB-C female pigtail socket | 1 | Black square block, 15.8 x 9.3 x 11 mm, 2 wires (red 5V, black GND). Lives in the mount, so the cable runs down the wall behind the puck. These often have no CC resistors, so a C-to-C cable may not power it. A USB-A to C cable always works. |
| Dupont jumpers or hook-up wire | ~8 | C3 to the driver board, button, power. |

## Printed parts

Printed in matte white PLA. Fuzzy skin on the dome gives the soft "chalk" finish.

| Part | Qty | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tray | 1 | The back half of the puck. Holds the boards, the motor cradle and the bottom halves of the gear bearings. |
| Dome | 1 | The front half. Lip into the tray, 3 screws. Caps the motor and the gear bearings. |
| Wand gear | 1 | 20T, module 1, 7.4 mm hex bore, with a journal each side that runs in the case. Slides along the wand. |
| Motor pinion | 1 | 20T, module 1, D bore. Press fit on the motor shaft. |
| Switch plug | 1 | Pushes in behind the switch so it can't be pressed into the case. |
| Mount | 1 | 90 mm drum, the same size as the puck, about 30 mm deep. Command strips on the back, holds the USB socket. |

## Hardware

All M3, socket head.

| Hardware | Qty | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M3 heat-set inserts | 6 | 3 in the dome, 1 in the tray centre, 2 in the board standoffs (4.2 mm pockets) |
| M3 x 20 | 3 | From the back of the tray into the dome |
| M3 x 8 | 1 | From the back of the mount into the tray, sets the angle |
| M3 x 6 | 2 | Driver board onto its standoffs |
| Command strips | 2 | Mount to the wall. Picture-hanging (interlocking) strips let it come off the wall to open it up. |
