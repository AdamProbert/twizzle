# Parts list

Everything that goes into one Twizzle.

## Electronics

| Part | Qty | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ESP32-C3 SuperMini | 1 | 18 x 22.5 mm. Sits flat in a frame on the tray floor. |
| 28BYJ-48 5 V stepper | 1 | From a 5-pack with ULN2003 boards. Snip the two mounting ears off, they hit the floor and the dome. |
| ULN2003 driver board (green) | 1 | About 35 x 32 mm, comes with the motor. Kept whole, on 4 standoffs. |
| 12 mm tactile switch with round cap | 1 | Front of the dome. |
| USB-C female pigtail socket | 1 | 15.8 x 9.3 x 11 mm, 2 wires (red 5V, black GND). These often have no CC resistors, so a C-to-C cable may not power it. A USB-A to C cable always works. |
| Dupont jumpers or hook-up wire | ~8 | C3 to the driver board, button, power. |

## Printed parts

Printed in matte white PLA. Fuzzy skin on the dome gives the soft "chalk" finish.

| Part | Qty | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tray | 1 | The back half. Holds everything. |
| Dome | 1 | The front half. Press-fit lip into the tray, plus 2 screws. |
| Wand gear | 1 | 20T, module 1, 7.4 mm hex bore. Slides along the wand. |
| Motor pinion | 1 | 20T, module 1, D bore. Press fit on the motor shaft. |
| Switch plug | 1 | Pushes in behind the switch so it can't be pressed into the case. |
| Spacer | 5 | 60 mm, 5 mm thick, toothed. |
| Base | 1 | 60 mm, 5 mm thick. Command strips go on the back. |
